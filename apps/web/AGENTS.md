## design-monogatari web

Next.js estimate app (port **3000**). FSD in `src/` (`_app` + `_pages/estimate`). Next `app/` only re-exports. UI from `@repo/ui/*`. Theme: `next-themes` + `html.light` / `html.dark`. The home route is the estimate wizard: `src/_pages/estimate/ui/` (server page decodes the URL, client `EstimateApp` runs the steps, price bar, summary and the confirm / blocked / suggestion dialogs; an automatic selection change is shown as one small chip over the bottom of the car (`ui/stage-notice.tsx`, replaced by the newest change, gone after 7s, never over the options) and listed under 자동으로 바뀐 항목 in the 견적 요약 sheet; an inline notice pushed the options down and a toast covered them and piled up per selection; unreadable URL values use one sticky error toast, `ui/restore-issue-toast.tsx`). The estimate rules live in `src/_pages/estimate/model/` as pure TypeScript (catalog data, `resolveSelection`, `calculateQuote`, URL codec, and the wizard reducer `wizard.ts` that turns resolutions into screen state) with vitest tests (`pnpm --filter web test`); rule `.claude/rules/estimate-domain.md`. The 3D car (`ui/vehicle-stage.tsx` → lazy `ui/vehicle-viewer.tsx` via `next/dynamic` `ssr: false`, from `@repo/vehicle-3d/viewer`) repaints with the selected exterior colour and follows the selected wheel size (19/20/21in via `model/wheel-map.ts`); the interior colour (`model/interior-map.ts`) recolours seats, doors, headliner and pillars to a similar colour; garnish and seat layout are not modelled. The brake caliper is a single-choice slot (`caliper`, on the 휠 step; 기본/블루/레드/블랙/옐로/골드 follow real makers' practice — BMW M Sport blue, Audi S/AMG/Lexus red, Porsche yellow/black, Polestar and carbon-ceramic gold — with invented prices; availability can depend on trim **and wheel** via `Condition.wheel`), painted in 3D by `model/caliper-map.ts`. The 휠 마감 (`rimFinish`, 기본 글로시 블랙/실버/오빗 그레이/브론즈, also on the 휠 step, rule by trim and wheel like the caliper) recolours only the rim in 3D (`model/rim-map.ts`). The URL `c` is 9 chars now (caliper, then rim finish last); `decodeEstimate` opens any length from 7 to 9 and fills the missing trailing slots with defaults (new slots must be appended to `SINGLE_SLOTS`, never inserted). The sunroof shows only when `model/roof-map.ts` `hasSunroof` is true (the 파노라마 선루프 or 스카이 라운지 루프 package is in the selection); otherwise the opening is closed. With a sunroof, a 선루프 열어 보기 switch (`SwitchField` with a rolling 닫힘/열림 word in a small translucent pill) sits over the top-left of the car and slides the glass open (state local to `VehicleStage`, reset when the package is removed). It overlays the car, so it takes no row space and nothing shifts. The same corner is one `Morph` frame shared with the interior note of the inside views: switching view morphs the pill into the note (and back), and the frame slides out when there is neither a sunroof nor an inside view. The camera follows the step (`model/stage-view.ts`: 휠 step → front wheel close-up, 시트 → cabin looking back, 내장 → driver's-eye dashboard, others → exterior) with a `Segmented` of the four views under the stage (full width below `lg`) for a manual pick that lasts only for that step; inside views have no zoom and show an overlay saying the interior is the model default (presets and limits: `packages/vehicle-3d/AGENTS.md` Camera views). It shows a skeleton while loading and a colour dot with a message when WebGL or the model is unavailable. 3D assets: `public/models/g05-web/*.glb` (optimized X5 parts, no emblem) + `public/models/studio.hdr`; terms in the root `NOTICE`.

**Layout** (`ui/estimate-app.tsx`, `ui/vehicle-stage.tsx`, `ui/estimate-header.tsx`, custom Tailwind variant `split` in `_app/styles/globals.css`, same media query as `SPLIT_QUERY` in `estimate-app.tsx`): the page itself never scrolls (`h-dvh`, `overflow-hidden`); only the option panel scrolls and its price bar is sticky at the bottom of that panel, so the car is always visible. `split` (width ≥ 1024px, or a short landscape screen such as a phone on its side) puts the car on the left and the panel on the right (480px, `min(30rem,56vw)` when narrower). Below it the car is on top: 34dvh, 18dvh after the panel is scrolled down more than 64px (back at 8px; it only shrinks when enough scroll length remains after shrinking, so it cannot flicker). Zoom in/out/reset (icon buttons, a column at the top-right of the car) and the four rotate arrows (a row at the bottom-right) are laid over the car at every width, so there is no sideways-scrolling control row (touch drag and pinch are not the only way). They are hidden while the car is in its shrunken state (`compact`), where they would not fit; `StageNotice` (the automatic-change chip) shows its whole sentence with no ellipsis: when the arrow plate is drawn (`data-tools` on the stage) the chip sits above it at nearly full width, leaving only the right side clear for the zoom plate; without the plate (shrunken stage, short touch screen) it sits at the bottom edge. The rotate arrows are also hidden on short touch screens (`short-touch` variant in `_app/styles/globals.css`: height ≤ 26rem and a coarse pointer, i.e. a phone held sideways), where the plates would cover about a third of a 240px-tall car; touch can drag to rotate, and mouse/keyboard screens keep them. The inside-view note shows only its first sentence (`INSIDE_VIEW_NOTE_APPROX`) so it stays one or two lines on a narrow stage; the zoom sentence (`INSIDE_VIEW_NOTE_NO_ZOOM`) shows from `xl`, and the screen-reader status always reads the whole note. The model credit (`StageNotes`) sits under the car from `lg` and at the end of the panel below it, never both. A step change scrolls the panel to the top. `print:hidden!` is needed on the stage wrapper because `split:flex` would otherwise win in print.

The result step has a 차량 이미지 저장 button (`ResultStep` `captureImage`, from `VehicleStage` `onCaptureChange` once the model is drawn): a 1920x1080 PNG of the current car (colour, wheel, rim, caliper, sunroof) from a fixed front 3/4 view on a light background, via `InspectHandle.capture`, downloaded as `X5-견적-<색상>.png`. The button is disabled while the model is loading or WebGL is unavailable.
