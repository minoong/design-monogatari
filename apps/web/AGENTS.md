## design-monogatari web

Next.js estimate app (port **3000**). FSD in `src/` (`_app`, `_pages/intro`, `_pages/estimate`, `shared/ui`). Next `app/` only re-exports. UI from `@repo/ui/*`. Theme: `next-themes` + `html.light` / `html.dark`. Routes: `/` is the intro (`src/_pages/intro/`, below), `/estimate` is the estimate wizard. Old shared links `/?c=…` (any of the query keys `v c p a r t step`) are redirected to `/estimate` with the query kept by `redirects()` in `next.config.js`, so the intro stays static. `src/shared/ui/theme-toggle.tsx` is used by both pages (pages may not import the app layer above them). The wizard: `src/_pages/estimate/ui/` (server page decodes the URL, client `EstimateApp` runs the steps, price bar, summary and the confirm / blocked / suggestion dialogs; an automatic selection change is shown as a sentence in the top-left `Morph` frame of the car (`ui/stage-notice.tsx` `useStageNotice`, replaces the interior note or the sunroof pill for about 4s and morphs back, replaced by the newest change, never over the options; the screen-reader status always reads it) and listed under 자동으로 바뀐 항목 in the 견적 요약 sheet; an inline notice pushed the options down and a toast covered them and piled up per selection; unreadable URL values use one sticky error toast, `ui/restore-issue-toast.tsx`). The estimate rules live in `src/_pages/estimate/model/` as pure TypeScript (catalog data, `resolveSelection`, `calculateQuote`, URL codec, and the wizard reducer `wizard.ts` that turns resolutions into screen state) with vitest tests (`pnpm --filter web test`); rule `.claude/rules/estimate-domain.md`. The 3D car (`ui/vehicle-stage.tsx` → lazy `ui/vehicle-viewer.tsx` via `next/dynamic` `ssr: false`, from `@repo/vehicle-3d/viewer`) repaints with the selected exterior colour and follows the selected wheel size (19/20/21in via `model/wheel-map.ts`); the interior colour (`model/interior-map.ts`) recolours seats, doors, headliner and pillars to a similar colour; garnish and seat layout are not modelled. The brake caliper is a single-choice slot (`caliper`, on the 휠 step; 기본/블루/레드/블랙/옐로/골드 follow real makers' practice — BMW M Sport blue, Audi S/AMG/Lexus red, Porsche yellow/black, Polestar and carbon-ceramic gold — with invented prices; availability can depend on trim **and wheel** via `Condition.wheel`), painted in 3D by `model/caliper-map.ts`. The 휠 마감 (`rimFinish`, 기본 글로시 블랙/실버/오빗 그레이/브론즈, also on the 휠 step, rule by trim and wheel like the caliper) recolours only the rim in 3D (`model/rim-map.ts`). The URL `c` is 9 chars now (caliper, then rim finish last); `decodeEstimate` opens any length from 7 to 9 and fills the missing trailing slots with defaults (new slots must be appended to `SINGLE_SLOTS`, never inserted). The sunroof shows only when `model/roof-map.ts` `hasSunroof` is true (the 파노라마 선루프 or 스카이 라운지 루프 package is in the selection); otherwise the opening is closed. With a sunroof, a 선루프 열어 보기 switch (`SwitchField` with a rolling 닫힘/열림 word in a small translucent pill) sits over the top-left of the car and slides the glass open (state local to `VehicleStage`, reset when the package is removed). It overlays the car, so it takes no row space and nothing shifts. The same corner is one `Morph` frame shared with the interior note of the inside views and the automatic-change sentence: the pill morphs into the note when the view changes (and back), into the sentence for about 4s after an automatic change, and the frame slides out when there is none of them. That keeps the car under three white boxes at most (frame, zoom plate, arrow plate) instead of stacking a separate chip on top. The camera follows the step (`model/stage-view.ts`: 휠 step → front wheel close-up, 시트 → cabin looking back, 내장 → driver's-eye dashboard, others → exterior) with a `Segmented` of the four views under the stage (full width below `lg`) for a manual pick that lasts only for that step; inside views have no zoom and show an overlay saying the interior is the model default (presets and limits: `packages/vehicle-3d/AGENTS.md` Camera views). It shows a skeleton while loading and a colour dot with a message when WebGL or the model is unavailable. 3D assets: `public/models/g05-web/*.glb` (optimized X5 parts, no emblem) + `public/models/studio.hdr`; terms in the root `NOTICE`.

**Intro** (`src/_pages/intro/`, route `/`): a dark cinematic product page modelled on Apple's and Polestar's product pages (rebuilt 2026-11-28 at the user's request after two earlier versions, text floating over a fixed live 3D car and then a configurator split, were rejected as dated). The whole page is the `dark` token scope; the spec, build and price sections hard-cut to `light` (`LIGHT_SCOPE` in `ui/intro-app.tsx`, a constant because `.light` is a token selector, not a Tailwind class). No live WebGL: the car is **pre-rendered frames** from the same 3D model, scrubbed by GSAP (`@repo/ui/scroll-image-sequence`), so the rule "page scroll never drives the 3D car" holds. Sections in order (`ui/intro-app.tsx`):

1. `IntroNav` (`ui/intro-nav.tsx`): sticky 52px local nav (`NAV_PX`), transparent at the top, `bg-background/80` + blur once scrolled; X5, the total (`AnimatedAmount`, `aria-hidden`) and a pill 견적 시작.
2. Hero: `ScrollImageSequence mode="play"` plays the `reveal` frames once (the car approaches from far front to front 3/4); `h1` and the 시작가 + 견적 시작하기 pill rise once by CSS (`intro-rise` in `_app/styles/globals.css`, 30px, 0.9s, 150ms steps, no JS needed for the first screen).
3. A statement (`SceneHeading`: `RevealGroup`/`Reveal`, 30px, 150ms stagger, once in view, Apple timing).
4. `OrbitScene` (`ui/intro-scenes.tsx`): pinned 3.2 screens under the nav; the title leaves at 4–9% (`leaveAt`: opacity and −32px), the `orbit` frames run over 8–74% (front 3/4 → a full turn → driver-side profile), then four callouts enter one by one from `lg`: a dot on the part, a vertical leader line into the empty space above or below the car (`side: 'up' | 'down'`, length in `cqh` of a 16:9 `FrameBox` that matches the `contain` image) and the label (`enterAt`/`growAt`; anchors measured on the last frame, `CALLOUTS`); below `lg` they are a two-column list at the bottom. Overlay helpers give `var(--sequence-progress, …)` a fallback so reduced motion shows the finished scene. These overlay effects are CSS `calc()` on the sequence's `--sequence-progress`, not React state.
5. Big-number specs (`SPECS` from the catalog, 72px, `tracking-tighter`, hairline + caption; Polestar's charging block).
6. `ApproachScene id="wheel"` (`fit="cover"`, the car leaves the frame and `contain` showed a hard edge): pinned, the camera closes in on the front wheel; a lead rises at 84%.
7. Build (light): `StillStack` (`ui/intro-configurator.tsx`) stacks pre-rendered colour × wheel stills (`next/image`) and crossfades the chosen one (new 170ms, old 80ms later; Polestar) next to `ConfiguratorPanel` (big rolling colour name, trim / colour / wheel controls from `ui/intro-sections.tsx`, always in the Tab order).
8. `ApproachScene id="cabin"` (`fit="cover"`): from above the car through the panoramic roof down to the driver's eye line (rendered with the studio environment as a dimmed, blurred background, `capture({environmentBackground})`, so the glass does not show a blank plane); the lead and the approximate-interior note rise at the end.
9. Interior colour: interior stills crossfade with the interior choices.
10. Price (light): the total, `IntroPrice`, 지금 사양으로 견적 시작하기; footer with the CC BY credit.
    Choices go through the estimate rules (`model/intro-selection.ts` `chooseInIntro`, notices per slot via `IntroNoticeSlot`); the 견적 시작 links carry the selection to `/estimate?…` (`encodeEstimate`); a `role="status"` line reads the total. Reduced motion: no pins or playback (each sequence shows its last frame, `restingProgress={1}`), reveals are instant. The intro reuses the estimate rules only through `_pages/estimate/index.model.ts`.

**Intro frames** (`public/intro/frames/<reveal|orbit|wheel|cabin>/<lg|sm>/NNNN.webp`, 1600×900 and 960×540, transparent; `public/intro/stills/lg/exterior-<color>-<wheel>.webp`, `interior-<interior>.webp`): shots, counts and camera paths are data in `src/_pages/intro/model/frame-shots.ts` (`SEQUENCES`, `poseAt`, world coordinates of `@repo/vehicle-3d`). To re-render after changing a path or the model: run `node apps/web/scripts/intro-frames-sink.mjs` (writes POSTed frames under `public/intro/`, localhost origins only), open `/dev/intro-frames` on the dev server (a dev-only route, `notFound()` in production; `?only=orbit,stills&size=lg` limits the batch), wait for 준비되었습니다 and press 저장 시작; it sets the look per shot, calls `InspectHandle.capture({camera, background: null, type: 'image/webp'})` for every frame and posts it. Re-measure the callout anchors on the last `orbit` frame if that path changes.

Look (decided with the user, do not undo): black page, the car large, big type, hard section colour cuts, motion that is pinned scrub or a single entrance. Dot fields, glows, ghost numerals, bordered cards and text floating over a live canvas read as AI output or dated.

The 3D credit sits in the page footer (CC BY 4.0).

**Layout** (`ui/estimate-app.tsx`, `ui/vehicle-stage.tsx`, `ui/estimate-header.tsx`, custom Tailwind variant `split` in `_app/styles/globals.css`, same media query as `SPLIT_QUERY` in `estimate-app.tsx`): the page itself never scrolls (`h-dvh`, `overflow-hidden`); only the option panel scrolls and its price bar is sticky at the bottom of that panel, so the car is always visible. Below `sm` (640px) the 이전 button in that bar becomes an icon button (`aria-label` 이전, same 48px height) on every step that also has a 다음 button; at 320px the total plus two labelled buttons overflowed the inner width by 1px and always wrapped to two rows. The result step has only 이전, so it keeps the labelled button. `split` (width ≥ 1024px, or a short landscape screen such as a phone on its side) puts the car on the left and the panel on the right (480px, `min(30rem,56vw)` when narrower). Below it the car is on top: 34dvh, 18dvh after the panel is scrolled down more than 64px (back at 8px; it only shrinks when enough scroll length remains after shrinking, so it cannot flicker). Zoom in/out/reset (icon buttons, a column at the top-right of the car) and the four rotate arrows (a row at the bottom-right) are laid over the car at every width, so there is no sideways-scrolling control row (drag and pinch are not the only way; the car also zooms with the mouse wheel and a two-finger pinch, `wheelZoom` on). They are hidden while the car is in its shrunken state (`compact`), where they would not fit. The rotate arrows are also hidden on short touch screens (`short-touch` variant in `_app/styles/globals.css`: height ≤ 26rem and a coarse pointer, i.e. a phone held sideways), where the plates would cover about a third of a 240px-tall car; touch can drag to rotate, and mouse/keyboard screens keep them. The inside-view note shows only its first sentence (`INSIDE_VIEW_NOTE_APPROX`) so it stays one or two lines on a narrow stage; the zoom sentence (`INSIDE_VIEW_NOTE_NO_ZOOM`) shows from `xl`, and the screen-reader status always reads the whole note. The model credit (`StageNotes`) sits under the car from `lg` and at the end of the panel below it, never both. A step change scrolls the panel to the top. `print:hidden!` is needed on the stage wrapper because `split:flex` would otherwise win in print.

처음부터 다시 견적 (result step) resets at once with no confirm dialog and offers 실행 취소 in a toast for 5s: the reducer keeps the pre-reset state in `WizardState.undo`, `ui/reset-undo-toast.tsx` shows the toast while `undo` exists, and any other action clears `undo` (so the toast closes and a later undo can never overwrite a new choice); history uses `replaceState`, so Back is not an undo.

The seven icon-only stage buttons (zoom in/out/reset at the top-right of the car, the four rotate arrows at the bottom-right) go through `StageIconButton` in `ui/vehicle-stage.tsx`, which gives the `aria-label` and the same text as a `Tooltip` (left for the zoom column, top for the rotate row; `TooltipProvider` is in the root layout `_app/index.tsx`). Tooltips never open on touch and a disabled button (inside views, zoom) opens none, so the inside-view note stays visible text.

The result step has a 차량 이미지 저장 button (`ResultStep` `captureImage`, from `VehicleStage` `onCaptureChange` once the model is drawn): a 1920x1080 PNG of the current car (colour, wheel, rim, caliper, sunroof) from a fixed front 3/4 view on a light background, via `InspectHandle.capture`, downloaded as `X5-견적-<색상>.png`. The button is disabled while the model is loading or WebGL is unavailable.
